# Laptop Cooler v2 – ROG Strix G513RM


## Features

- Designed to fit the previous-gen ROG Strix chassis, would fit most laptops out there.
- Supports up to three 120mm fans
- Pressure-focused airflow design

## Printing sequence

Print the pieces in the orientation seen in the pictures attached. Big pieces are designed to interlock with each 
other but there is no added tolerance as my test prints showed it works best that way. If you don't 
have access to precise 3d printer you may want to add some tolerance to the connection points or try 
to sand down the connection points if it doesn't fit. 

## Required Components

- 1x On/off switch (2 cm × 1.3 cm slot)
- 1x Female DC barrel jack (1.1 cm slot)
- Up to 3x 120 mm cooling fans (12V recommended)
- Jumper wires (make sure they are thick enough; very thin wires may overheat)
- Sealing foam (should not be too stiff, otherwise it will not conform to the laptop surface and may leave air gaps)
- Sticky putty or mounting tack (for sealing purposes)
- Some kind of glue
- 4 cm × 4 mm screws (for mounting the fans)
- 12V DC power supply (compatible with the selected DC barrel jack)
     
3d printed parts:

- 4 big main body parts
- mesh covers (per fan)
- fan hole cover (per unused fan slot)
- 2 big screw access hole plug
- 4 small screw access hole plug

## Assembly

When assembling the cooler, start by fitting the pieces that have the most connection points.  
If you assemble the simpler sections first, it may become harder to align and fit the remaining parts properly.
There are screw access holes on the body. These are necessary to mount the fans properly,
as there would not be enough working space otherwise. After mounting the fans, seal these holes using the included plug pieces.
For better airflow sealing, applying some sticky putty around the plug pieces is recommended.
